## Animated launcher scenes

`overworld.jpg`, `nether.jpg`, `end.jpg` and `sift.jpg` are original project illustrations generated
with the built-in ImageGen tool on October 19, 2026, then encoded as JPEG at
quality 86 using macOS ImageIO through `sips`. Each is 1672 × 941 pixels; the
files are 667,489, 548,275, 390,036 and 599,049 bytes respectively. They contain no baked-in text
or game UI. The native launcher supplies camera drift, particles and scene fades
in code; these images are not video or gameplay captures. Original voxel actors
in `src/launcher/Adventure.swift` provide the moving creatures and portal

The README's original `poster.webp` is unchanged. The four scene assets are
individually allowlisted for source export and copied into the native app

## Poster prompt

Create an ambitious, exceptionally polished original Minecraft-style promotional
poster for an unofficial open-source project named Minecraft Bedrock for Mac.
Landscape 16:9 poster composition, visually bold and memorable like premium voxel
game key art. EXACT readable typography only: large headline 'BEDROCK' and on the
next line 'ON YOUR MAC'; small footer 'UNOFFICIAL SOURCE PREVIEW'. Do not render
any other text. The headline is physical extruded block lettering made of pale
stone with crisp, confident geometric forms, perfectly readable at thumbnail
size. A spectacular lush voxel world fills the image: grass-topped cliffs,
blocky pine and oak forests, an aquamarine waterfall that falls into a blue
river, a distant angular mountain under a luminous early morning sky. In the
foreground, an elegant open silver laptop acts as a portal into the block world,
with the landscape spilling naturally from the screen and onto a few blocks
beside it. A single small original blocky explorer silhouette with a pickaxe
provides scale; no recognisable copyrighted character or official logos.
Integrated composition rather than separate cards, dashboard panels or a stock
laptop product photo. Natural emerald greens, glacier blues, warm sunlit stone
and silver metal. Cinematic depth, precise crafted voxel geometry, warm rim
lighting, rich but controlled atmosphere, sharp details and beautiful negative
space around the headline. Make the poster feel like an invitation to explore.
No Apple logo, Microsoft logo, official Minecraft logo, fake game interface,
download button, performance or FPS claim. It is clearly original project
artwork, not a gameplay screenshot. The output is one finished high-impact
poster that does most of the storytelling visually
